role Scrum Master
period February – June 2026
type Team Project
  • HTML
  • CSS

The Problem

Griya Raden Saleh relied on manual records and Excel spreadsheets to manage room information and bookings. Meanwhile, prospective tenants had to contact the property through WhatsApp to ask about room availability, facilities, and rental details, making information access and booking management less efficient.

The Solution

Griya Raden Saleh is a web-based boarding house management and booking platform designed to simplify room information access and booking processes. The website allows prospective tenants to explore room details, facilities, and availability, while providing administrators with tools to manage room data, bookings, and payment verification in one centralized system.

The Impact & Key Learnings

Griya Raden Saleh provides a centralized platform for prospective tenants to access boarding house information and submit bookings online. It also streamlines administrative tasks by organizing room data, booking records, and payment verification in one system, reducing reliance on manual record-keeping.

  • Gained experience in applying the Scrum framework to a software development project.
  • Learned to coordinate team tasks and monitor project progress through structured sprints.
  • Improved understanding of system requirements and the development of web-based management solutions.

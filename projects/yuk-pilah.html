Yuk Pilah

  • HTML

The Problem

Limited public awareness and engagement in proper waste management highlight the need for accessible environmental education and interactive digital solutions.

The Solution

Yuk Pilah is a digital waste management and education platform featuring interactive learning materials, a CO₂ calculator, an action map, challenges, a leaderboard, and a chatbot to encourage sustainable habits.

The Impact & Key Learnings

Yuk Pilah promoted environmental awareness and encouraged users to adopt more sustainable waste management habits through accessible education and interactive features. The platform also received Good to Excellent UX evaluation results from 25 respondents aged 17–25, with a Novelty score of 1.320.

  • Gained experience in developing web-based solutions and integrating interactive features.
  • Applied gamification concepts to encourage sustainable habits.
  • Evaluated user experience through structured feedback.
